# Resolution Method Literature Basis

The solution-method taxonomy is intentionally broader than the currently
executable implementation.

Key review anchors include:

- Brahimi, Dauzère-Pérès, Najid & Nordli,
  *Single item lot sizing problems*, EJOR 168(1), 2006:
  solution methods and mathematical formulations for uncapacitated and
  capacitated single-item lot sizing;

- Jans & Degraeve,
  *Meta-heuristics for dynamic lot sizing: A review and comparison of solution
  approaches*, EJOR 177(3), 2007:
  metaheuristics plus discussion of dynamic programming, cutting planes,
  Dantzig-Wolfe decomposition, Lagrangian relaxation and dedicated heuristics;

- Buschkühl, Sahling, Helber & Tempelmeier,
  *Dynamic capacitated lot sizing problems: A classification and review of
  solution approaches*, OR Spectrum 32(2), 2010.

Catalog entries are family-level scientific vocabulary. Concrete future
algorithm adapters must preserve the assumptions of their original papers.
